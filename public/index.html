    How it works
    🎯
    Goal

    Grow the longest, highest-scoring snake and climb the leaderboard. You gain length and points by eating — the longer you survive, the bigger you get.

    🪙
    Getting in

    Enter a name, paste your Solana wallet (that's where rewards are sent), pick a skin, and hit PLAY. Free to play in demo mode.

    💰
    Win real SOL

    Every 3 minutes the round ends and the top 5 snakes split creator-fee SOL, paid straight to their wallets.

    🍬
    Eat to grow

    Swallow the glowing dots scattered across the map. Gold orbs are worth +5 each — well worth chasing down.

    ⚔️
    Kill rivals

    You only die if your head hits another snake's body — so cut in front of rivals and make them crash into you. Their corpse drops loot worth about half their size for you to gobble up.

    🎮
    Controls

    Steer with mouse or touch. Hold click / space / the BOOST button to sprint — boosting burns a little of your length, so use it wisely.

    🧱
    The edge is lethal

    The glowing red boundary kills on contact — don't let anyone corner you into it.

    🛡️
    Spawn shield

    You're briefly invincible (cyan ring) right after spawning, so you won't get instantly killed in a crowd.

    👑
    Crown & resets

    The biggest snake on screen wears a 👑. The whole arena wipes clean for a fresh competition every 3 rounds.
